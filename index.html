About

Profile

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree at the Institute of Science Tokyo. My research focuses on designing efficient AI systems that can perform high-quality inference under limited computational and memory resources.

My interests span neural network compression, quantization, retrieval-augmented and memory-augmented models, approximate attention, and combinatorial optimization. I aim to develop methods that make advanced AI models more practical, scalable, and deployable.
