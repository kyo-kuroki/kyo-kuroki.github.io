About

About

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree at the Institute of Science Tokyo. My research focuses on designing efficient AI systems that can perform high-quality inference under limited computational and memory resources. My interests span neural network compression, quantization, retrieval-augmented and memory-augmented models, approximate attention, and combinatorial optimization.

Research

  • Efficient Deep Learning Systems Efficient inference, model compression, and deployment-friendly learning systems for large-scale neural networks.
  • Neural Network Quantization Optimization-based quantization and matrix compression for reducing memory and computation while preserving performance.
  • External Memory & Retrieval Retrieval-augmented and memory-augmented inference that improves adaptation without expanding model parameters.
  • Combinatorial Optimization Classical optimization algorithms and annealing-inspired methods for large-scale discrete optimization problems.
